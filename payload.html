02 / The payload

Less atmosphere.
More to discover.

An air-intake prototype for ABEP research. A focused experiment in very low Earth orbit.

↳Air intake only. No onboard propulsion.
50° 51′ N
04° 21′ E

A very thin atmosphere.

The mission targets very low Earth orbit (VLEO), around 200 km in the initial concept. The exact orbit remains to be confirmed.

Target environment: VLEO

Make every molecule count.

The prototype explores the collection and compression of residual atmospheric particles. The geometry and performance targets will be added as the design develops.

Research focus: collection efficiency

Understand the intake.

The goal is to investigate intake performance on a 3U CubeSat. The measurement approach, sensors and validation plan are still to be confirmed.

Mission scope: no propulsion

ABEP = Atmosphere-Breathing Electric Propulsion. Our project studies the air-intake part only.
