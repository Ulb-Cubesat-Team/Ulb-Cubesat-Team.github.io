Mission

Payload and spacecraft

An air-intake experiment for atmosphere-breathing electric propulsion research, carried by a 3U CubeSat.

PayloadSpacecraft

Payload

ABEP air-intake prototype

The payload is an air-intake prototype for atmosphere-breathing electric propulsion research. The experiment studies the collection of residual atmospheric particles in very low Earth orbit.

Scope: air intake only. No onboard propulsion.

A very thin atmosphere

The mission targets very low Earth orbit (VLEO), around 200 km in the initial concept. The final orbit has not been fixed yet.

Collecting residual gas

The prototype is designed to collect and compress particles from the residual atmosphere. Its geometry and performance targets are being defined as the design progresses.

Measuring intake performance

The goal is to characterise the intake on a 3U CubeSat. The measurement approach, sensors and validation plan are currently being developed.

ABEP stands for Atmosphere-Breathing Electric Propulsion. This mission studies the air intake only.

Spacecraft

Spacecraft design

A 3U CubeSat platform built around the air-intake experiment. The design is currently at concept stage.

Platform overview

The spacecraft is at concept stage and its configuration is not final. Detailed design information will be published once it has been reviewed.
