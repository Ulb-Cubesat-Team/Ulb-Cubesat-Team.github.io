Studying air intakes in very low Earth orbit

The ULB CubeSat Team is a group of engineering students developing a 3U CubeSat that will carry an air-intake prototype for atmosphere-breathing electric propulsion (ABEP) research.

Mission

Why very low Earth orbit?

Around 200 km above the Earth, a thin residual atmosphere remains. ABEP concepts aim to collect this gas and use it as propellant, which could let spacecraft operate for long periods at altitudes where drag would otherwise bring them down quickly.

Our mission focuses on the first stage of that chain: the air intake. The CubeSat carries an intake prototype to study how residual atmospheric particles can be collected. The satellite itself has no onboard propulsion.

Read about the payload

Objectives

  1. Collection

    Investigate how an intake captures particles from the residual atmosphere.

  2. Performance

    Evaluate the behaviour of an ABEP air-intake prototype.

  3. Integration

    Design and integrate the experiment on a 3U CubeSat platform.
